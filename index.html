Valuation Scorecard — Pabrai Scale

A watchlist ranked on Mohnish Pabrai's rule: sell only when a name is egregiously overvalued, not merely fully valued. PEG is the key lens, a high multiple justified by growth (low PEG) is a hold; a high multiple the growth can't justify (PEG ~4+) is a sell candidate. Tiers run most overvalued to cheapest. Metrics as of 2026-06-30; unprofitable names (dashes in PE columns) are judged on price-to-sales.

Egregious names, valued 4 years out (2030 P/E)

Fast growers get mis-valued 4 years out because the Street stops modeling at ~2-3 years. Each egregious name's P/E on derived 2030 EPS (non-GAAP where relevant), consensus vs a sourced bull case. No analyst publishes a clean 2030 EPS, so these are extrapolations (~±15-20%), with sources linked. Takeaway: on 2030 numbers, HIMS is genuinely cheap, ARM/NET/CRWD compress into the 50s on the bull case, and TSLA's bull is a robotaxi lottery ticket.
