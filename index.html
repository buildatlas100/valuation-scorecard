Valuation Scorecard — Pabrai Scale

A watchlist ranked on Mohnish Pabrai's rule: sell only when a name is egregiously overvalued, not merely fully valued. PEG is the key lens, a high multiple justified by growth (low PEG) is a hold; a high multiple the growth can't justify (PEG ~4+) is a sell candidate. Tiers run most overvalued to cheapest. Metrics as of 2026-09-19; "Egregious at" targets recomputed 2026-09-03 from live prices/multiples (tier review 2026-09-19: HOOD back to Overvalued on a 2.1 PEG after a 12% run, NOW to Cheap on a 0.98 PEG, and VST and ZETA rated Cheap on first full metrics; the egregious tier is unchanged: TSLA, ARM, NET, CRWD); unprofitable names (dashes in PE columns) are judged on price-to-sales.

Each row combines valuation (Fwd PE / PEG / P/S), quality (gross margin, FCF yield = free cash flow ÷ market cap, Rule of 40 = growth% + FCF-margin% for software where ≥40 is healthy / "n/m" for non-software or one-off-distorted like MU's memory supercycle), and trend (RSI 14-day; vs 200-day MA = how stretched, deep red stretched / teal below trend; 52-wk high = near 0 is peak froth, deeply negative is beaten down). Not financial advice, personal opinion for discussion. Multiples rounded; forward and 2030 figures are consensus/derived estimates and carry real uncertainty. "Egregious at" = the price where a name would cross into the egregious tier, recomputed 2026-09-03 from live prices and multiples with estimates held constant: fwd PE hits ~100, PEG hits ~4.5, or P/S hits ~35 on sub-40% growth, whichever comes first. It is a tripwire for tier promotion, not a price target; if earnings grow into the price, the tripwire moves up. Megacaps (>$2T) get a second, binding constraint: "cap-gravity" = the price where market cap would hit ~$7T (~12% of the S&P 500, beyond any single-stock weight ever sustained); the lower rule wins, since a 100x-forward NVDA would be a $30T+ company. Cyclicals and no-earnings names get a judgment note. Technicals as of 2026-09-19.
